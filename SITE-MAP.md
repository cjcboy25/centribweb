# Website structure

- `/` — home / company overview
- `/services/` — core services + process
- `/projects/` — full project portfolio
- `/blog/` — SEO content hub
- `/blog/<slug>/` — individual SEO article
- `/contact/` — inquiry/contact details

Excluded for now:
- Organizational Chart
- Permits and Certifications

## Content sources

Company facts and portfolio entries are based on the supplied 2026 company profile.
