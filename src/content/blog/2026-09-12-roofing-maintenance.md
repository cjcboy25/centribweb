A roof protects the building from weather exposure, so small defects are worth documenting early.

## Look for visible damage

Inspect accessible areas for damaged roofing sheets, loose or deteriorated flashing, visible gaps, corrosion, or areas where water appears to collect.

## Check gutters and drainage

Blocked or poorly draining gutters can contribute to water intrusion and staining. Keep drainage paths clear and observe how water moves during heavy rain.

## Watch interior signs

Water stains, damp finishes, peeling paint, and recurring leaks can indicate an issue that should be investigated rather than repeatedly covered up.

## Address the underlying cause

Repair work should identify the source of the problem and coordinate related roofing, flashing, waterproofing, masonry, or other works as required by the actual site condition.
