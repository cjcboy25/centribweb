Renovation projects can involve several trades at once. Before work starts, document what is changing and what must remain.

## Interior works

List spaces affected by demolition, carpentry, ceilings, flooring, tiling, painting, electrical, and plumbing works.

## Exterior works

For exterior upgrades, review walls, waterproofing, roofing, drainage, gates, and other exposed elements as applicable to the project.

## Existing-condition documentation

Photos and a simple room-by-room or area-by-area scope list can help establish the starting condition and identify items requiring attention.

## Coordination

When multiple trades are involved, sequencing matters. The work should be coordinated so one activity does not unnecessarily delay another.
