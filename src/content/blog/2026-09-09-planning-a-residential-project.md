Starting a residential construction project is easier when the scope is clear before mobilization. A structured planning stage helps align the owner, contractor, schedule, resources, and expected deliverables.

## 1. Define the project scope

Start with the type of work: new construction, extension, renovation, restoration, or a specialized package such as roofing. Identify the areas and systems included in the work.

## 2. Organize project information

Plans, reference photos, site information, material preferences, and known constraints should be gathered in one place. This reduces repeated clarification during execution.

## 3. Establish a realistic schedule

A useful schedule identifies major stages, dependencies, procurement requirements, and target completion dates. The Centribuild approach emphasizes disciplined scheduling and site coordination.

## 4. Plan communication

Clear reporting and open discussions make it easier to surface changes, decisions, and site concerns early.

## 5. Focus on value, not short-term cost

Construction decisions should consider durability, functionality, workmanship, and long-term maintenance—not only the initial price.
